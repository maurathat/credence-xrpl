# Credence build plan vs XRPL protocol (Phase C)

The plan was not edited. Line numbers refer to `docs/spec/credence-build-plan.md` at `checkpoint-0`.
Decision references are to `docs/decisions/0001-foundational-architecture.md`.

Each assertion gets two separate columns:
- **Source review:** SUPPORTED, SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION, UNSUPPORTED or CONTRADICTED, based on the
  rippled 3.4.1/3.4.0 source and official docs in `EXPECTATIONS.md`.
- **Run evidence:** what the implementer's spike demonstrated. Only E1 ran, so almost every row
  is NOT TESTED.

A protocol-source classification is not a run result. The plan's own exit criterion (line 507)
requires run evidence.

## Focus areas

| # | Plan assertion (line) | Source review | Run evidence | Basis and required condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | "LendingProtocolV1_1 restricts all loans to closed-ended vaults. Phases change automatically at dates set when the vault is created, and the dates cannot be changed later" (282; decision 3) | SUPPORTED | NOT TESTED | Enforced at `LoanBrokerSet` (open-ended vault returns `tecNO_PERMISSION`). Phase is computed from ledger time. Dates are immutable (EXPECTATIONS Q3) |
| P2 | Phase table: Subscription deposit/withdraw yes, loans no; Investment deposit/withdraw no, loans yes; Redemption withdraw yes, deposit/loans no; repayments yes in all (284-288) | SUPPORTED | NOT TESTED | Matches source and docs exactly. "Repayments in Subscription" is vacuous: no loan can exist before Investment |
| P3 | "create the vault with a subscription window of a few minutes, an investment phase long enough for the whole loan life… each run creates a fresh vault" (290) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION | NOT TESTED | `RedemptionDate - SubscriptionDate >= 180 s`. Both dates must be in the future when `VaultCreate` applies (else `tecEXPIRED`). Phases follow **ledger close time**, not wall clock (Q4) |
| P4 | "Confirm in the spike how phase dates behave on a standalone ledger whose ledgers you close by script" (290, 606) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION (answer known from source) | Partly: E1 shows a back-to-back close at exactly parent + 1 s | The phase clock is the parent close time. A boundary takes effect only after a ledger closes past it. Each scripted close adds at least 1 s, so rapid closing runs ledger time ahead of wall clock. `LoanSet.StartDate` is the close time of the ledger that includes it, later than the provisional value at submit (Q4) |
| P5 | Investment-only loan origination; "Loan in the wrong phase… Rejected… Ledger" (300, 483) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION | NOT TESTED | `tecTOO_SOON` in Subscription, `tecEXPIRED` in Redemption. **Condition:** these are `tec` results, so the tx is included, the fee is charged and the sequence consumed. "Rejected" means no Loan is created, not that the tx stays off-ledger |
| P6 | "make the final payment fall inside the vault's investment phase" (229) | SUPPORTED (protocol-enforced) | NOT TESTED | LoanSet requires `StartDate + PaymentInterval x PaymentTotal + 60 s <= RedemptionDate`, else `tecNO_PERMISSION` |
| P7 | Repayments across phases (288, 304) | SUPPORTED | NOT TESTED | `LoanPay` has no phase check. A payment that runs late into Redemption needs `tfLoanLatePayment` |
| P8 | Borrower: "Signer list: ResearchBot key + Credence policy key, quorum 2 (both must sign); regular key disabled" (264; decision 1) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION | NOT TESTED (E5 not run) | The 2-of-2 is enforced for multisigned transactions. **"Regular key disabled" is not a ledger state**; it is just the absence of `RegularKey`. The quorum can add one with `SetRegularKey`, and can also replace the list with `SignerListSet`. The policy signer must refuse to co-sign `SetRegularKey`, `SignerListSet` and `AccountSet` (the digest rule at 447 covers this only if the engine never approves those types) |
| P9 | "every exit from the borrower account carries two signatures" (101); "The agent cannot spend without Credence… enforced by the ledger itself" (258, 275) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION | NOT TESTED | True for every multisigned exit, including LoanPay. The owner's master key is the intended single-signature exception (Q14) |
| P10 | LoanSet: "two separate authorizations must both hold: the borrower account's own signing rules, and the Loan Broker's counterparty signature on the exact terms. Principal is disbursed to the borrower account" (300) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION (separation CONFIRMED; 2-of-2 `Account` composition LIKELY) | NOT TESTED (E6/E7 not run) | `Account` is checked by normal `checkSign`; `CounterpartySignature` is checked against the counterparty account. **Condition 1:** role separation of the signed bytes needs `fixCleanup3_4_0` (enabled in the spike config). **Condition 2:** the broker signature covers every non-signature field, including `Fee` (which depends on both signer counts: 4 x base for 2-of-2 + single broker), `Sequence` and `LastLedgerSequence`, so all must be fixed before the broker signs. **Condition 3:** disbursement is `PrincipalRequested - LoanOriginationFee`, and the fee goes to the broker owner. No upstream test shows a *positive* LoanSet with a multisigned `Account` plus a counterparty signature, so this remains the central untested item |
| P11 | Loan Broker counterparty signature: "Supplies the counterparty signature on a loan only when the owner's Ledger approval is present and valid" (87); "Debt without approval… loan never exists… Loan Broker signer" (482) | SUPPORTED | NOT TESTED | No LoanSet succeeds without a valid `CounterpartySignature` (`temBAD_SIGNER` if absent; altered terms are rejected at submit). Owner approval is enforced **off-ledger** by the Loan Broker signer, not by the ledger |
| P12 | ResearchBot "Cannot: Take on new debt without owner approval; disable the master key" (64) | Disable master: SUPPORTED. New debt: SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION | NOT TESTED | A multisigned `AccountSet asfDisableMaster` returns `tecNEED_MASTER_KEY`. The ledger *accepts* a LoanSet signed by the ResearchBot + policy quorum with the broker's counterparty signature, with no owner signature. "Without owner approval" therefore holds only while Credence's Loan Broker signer refuses. Against ResearchBot alone it holds; against ResearchBot and Credence together it does not |
| P13 | Facility approval, preferred: "the device signs the borrower side of the loan directly" (299) vs exit criterion "LoanSet with a 2-of-2 borrower" (507) and the risk fallback "Owner's Ledger signs loan transactions as master key" (576) | **Plan ambiguity, reported and not resolved** | NOT TESTED | One LoanSet's borrower side is *either* a master/regular single signature *or* a `Signers` multisig, never both ("Cannot both single- and multi-sign"). If the device signs the borrower side, that LoanSet is not 2-of-2 authorized. If the 2-of-2 signs, the device is not on-ledger. Maura needs to decide which LoanSet authorization the product uses. Both are protocol-valid |
| P14 | Owner master-key escape hatch: "the master key on the Ledger can act alone, change the signer list, or remove the agent instantly"; "Credence offline… owner sweeps funds with the Ledger" (277-278, 491; decision 1) | SUPPORTED | NOT TESTED | An enabled master key signs alone regardless of the signer list. Only the master key can set `lsfDisableMaster`. "Instantly" means the next closed ledger. Whether the Ledger device can sign these types is a separate spike (line 337) |
| P15 | Revocation: "the owner removes ResearchBot from the signer list. Every later spend fails on-ledger, before Credence is even asked" (305, 326, 480) | SUPPORTED WITH CONDITION | NOT TESTED | **Condition:** a replacement list must not let the policy key reach quorum alone, or Credence could then spend alone (contradicting 65 and 276). Delete the list (`SignerQuorum 0`, allowed while the master key is enabled) or replace ResearchBot with a new agent key at quorum 2. After deletion, an outstanding loan can only be repaid with the master key |
| P16 | Repayment scheduler: "computes what the ledger will accept now: the scheduled payment when due, or an early or full payment only if the loan's flags allow it" (84, 236) | CONTRADICTED (as worded) | NOT TESTED | Paying whole installments early and a full early payment (`tfLoanFullPayment`) need **no** loan flag. Only overpayment needs the loan's `lsfLoanOverpayment`, set by `tfLoanOverpayment` on LoanSet. A late installment must carry `tfLoanLatePayment`. Bounded correction: the scheduler pays `k x (roundUp(PeriodicPayment, LoanScale) + LoanServiceFee)` for whole k, a full payment, or a flagged overpayment if the loan allows it |
| P17 | Spike instruction: "make an early partial payment, and inspect principal, interest, fees and the next amount due" (239; exit criterion 507: "both scheduled and early LoanPay") | CONTRADICTED (partial) | NOT TESTED | There is **no sub-installment partial payment**: an amount below one installment returns `tecINSUFFICIENT_PAYMENT`. The ledger has **no** "interest" or "next amount due" field. Interest outstanding = `TotalValueOutstanding - PrincipalOutstanding`, and the next amount due must be computed (Q13). "Early LoanPay" is testable as early whole installments or full payment |
| P18 | "Impermissible repayment… Scheduler sends only the permitted amount" (490); "Anything earmarked but not yet payable stays in the account" (237) | SUPPORTED | NOT TESTED | Sub-installment amounts are rejected. Excess over whole installments is not taken unless it is a flagged overpayment on an overpayment-enabled loan |

## Bounded corrections Maura may want to make (reviewer did not apply them)

1. P13: decide whether the borrower side of LoanSet is the owner's device (master key) or the
   2-of-2 quorum, and make line 299, the exit criterion at 507 and the risk at 576 consistent.
2. P16/P17: replace "early partial payment" and "only if the loan's flags allow it" with the
   protocol's payment types (whole installments, full early, flagged overpayment, late). Note that
   interest outstanding and next amount due are computed, not read.
3. P22: qualify the first-loss statement with the configured cover rates.
4. P8/P15: specify revocation as deleting or replacing the list without leaving the policy key
   at quorum. State that "regular key disabled" is an off-ledger invariant the policy signer must
   protect.
5. P21: the Loan Broker account must also be the vault owner.
6. P25: specify the ledger-amount to micro-unit mapping (or choose an MPT tRLUSD).
