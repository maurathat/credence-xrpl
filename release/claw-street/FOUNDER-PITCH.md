## Script

I'm Maura, building Claw Street at Kessai Labs, powered by Credence.

AI agents can already call tools. But when an agent buys inference, who decides what it may
buy, which provider qualifies and what evidence counts as delivery?

Claw Street makes those terms explicit. The agent chooses under spending, credit and evidence
policies. Credence checks the offers, the agent signs the purchase outside the browser, and
Credence verifies the receipt before recording the charge.

Our working local demo shows that whole loop with offline mock providers: policy selection,
an agent-signed purchase, delivery evidence, response retrieval and facility accounting.

The public website lets you explore a browser simulation. XRPL marketplace payments and
hardware signing are future integrations; this demo sends no payments.

We're building procurement agents can explain, and owners can inspect. That's Claw Street —
Powered by Credence.
